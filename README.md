# CouchMode

A World of Warcraft interface made for playing comfortably from the couch.
Readable panels, controller controls, and less clutter help you keep your eyes
on the game.

Use the modules you like and turn off the rest. Made for Retail and WoW: Forever;
ConsolePort is optional.

## Modules

| Module | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Bags** | Organizes your inventory into categories, with search and compact or full views. |
| **Merchant** | Makes vendor items easier to browse, search, buy, and buy back. |
| **Player Bars** | Shows your health, mana, and other resources in bars you can place where you want. |
| **Cast Bar & Cast Ring** | Tracks your casts and channels with a bar or circular display. |
| **Party** | Shows party portraits, health, resources, buffs, debuffs, and group status icons. |
| **Nameplates** | Customizes names, health bars, and casting information above players and NPCs. |
| **Map** | Gives the minimap and world map a cleaner look, with an expandable minimap and useful location labels. |
| **Tips** | Shows information about players and NPCs you target or hover over, including quest objectives. |
| **Chat** | Makes conversations easier to follow with speaker portraits, grouped messages, channel filters, and chat history. |
| **Chat Bubbles** | Gives NPC speech bubbles portraits and animated text. |
| **Toasts** | Shows loot, money, currency, reputation, and system messages in a fading feed, with a filter for repetitive errors. |

## Getting started

1. Install the **CouchMode** folder in your game's `Interface/AddOns` folder and
   enable it on the character selection screen.
2. Type **`/cm`** to open settings and choose your modules.
3. Use **Edit Layout** to move and resize supported panels.
4. Try a module's **Preview** to see it without waiting for a party, conversation,
   or reward.

Open each module's settings to adjust its appearance and behavior.
Panel positions follow your active Edit Mode layout.

## Chat with a controller

Assign **CouchMode → Chat Composer** in Key Bindings to open the on-screen
keyboard, quick replies, and emote wheel. You can also open it from
**Chat → Quick Replies** in `/cm`.

Write a message or choose a saved reply, pick a channel or whisper recipient,
then send. Mouse and keyboard input work too.
